🔬 ESU Breathable Steel - Acoustic Analysis

Analyze the frequency-dependent acoustic properties of ESU Breathable Steel with different pore sizes. This interactive tool shows how the material behaves as a frequency-selective acoustic membrane, displaying reflection, absorption, and transmission coefficients across the audio spectrum.

Key Features:

  • Interactive Bode Plots: Standard audio engineering frequency scale (30 Hz - 20 kHz)
  • Pore Size Comparison: Analyze 30-50, 50-80, and 80-100 μm pore ranges
  • Adjustable Thickness: See how layer thickness affects acoustic resonances
  • Energy Conservation: Visualize how sound energy is reflected, absorbed, or transmitted
  • Material Optimization: Find the ideal configuration for your acoustic application
