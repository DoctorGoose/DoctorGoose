About the Founder

Jordan Weaver, PhD


Dr. Weaver is a dynamic and driven chemical engineer with a deep passion for unraveling the complexities of the immune system through data analysis and machine learning. With a PhD in Chemical Engineering from the University of Pittsburgh, Dr. Weaver specializes in mechanistic modeling, predictive analytics, and biomanufacturing processes.


Dr. Weaver's career is marked by a blend of academic excellence and practical industrial experience. As a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center, Dr. Weaver has made significant strides in understanding viral infections, particularly influenza and SARS-CoV-2.


With a strong foundation in data analysis, systems biology, and biomanufacturing, Dr. Weaver is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what is possible in the intersection of engineering and biological sciences.
